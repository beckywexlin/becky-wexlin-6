Funny t-shirts that survive the second look

Most funny graphic tees are funny exactly once — on the rack, for about four seconds. After that you own a shirt that explains a joke to strangers for the rest of its natural life. This collection is built the other way around. Every funny t-shirt here is made to be worn for years by someone who already thinks it's funny, not to ambush a passer-by in a parking lot.

Why these funny graphic tees are different

There are two kinds of humour shirts. One is a slogan in Impact font slapped onto whatever blank was cheapest that week. The other is a design somebody actually laboured over — kerning, weight, composition, the whole irritating business — for a joke that did not deserve that much effort. We only make the second kind.

  • Original artwork. Every graphic is drawn in-house in Santa Barbara. Nothing is licensed, scraped, or bought off a clip-art marketplace, which is why you won't find these designs anywhere else.
  • Deadpan beats loud. No Comment. and Rule #62 work because they under-sell. The shirt doesn't shout the punchline; it lets you deliver it.
  • Premium blanks only. Comfort Colors, Bella + Canvas, AS Colour and Gildan Softstyle, chosen per design. A joke that cracks after three washes stops being a joke.
  • Range, not one gag repeated. Bad Ass Tee is a pun. Gutter Rat Bowling is a whole invented league. Total Geebags Only. is a border policy.

Who this collection is for

  • People who have been told "you're so random" as though it were a criticism
  • Anyone shopping for the friend who already owns everything and likes nothing
  • Chronic over-thinkers who want a funny shirt that isn't embarrassing at 34
  • Group-chat historians, bit-committers, and the last person to leave the party
  • Gift-buyers who need a present that gets a genuine reaction, not a polite one

How to style a funny t-shirt (and how to gift one)

One loud thing at a time. If the graphic is doing the talking, everything else should shut up: straight-leg denim, plain outerwear, boots or beaters. Size up once if you want the boxy vintage drape, stay true-to-size for a normal fit — full measurements are on the size guide, and there's a longer breakdown in our guide to styling a graphic tee with jeans.

As a gift, funny tees punch far above their price because they prove you were paying attention. Pick the joke that belongs to them — the running one, the specific one — rather than the broadly funny one. Hot Mess Express for the friend who is late to everything, Support Your Local Library for the one with 40 overdue holds. More ideas in the best graphic tee gifts guide.

Funny t-shirt FAQ

Are these funny t-shirts actually funny, or dad-at-a-barbecue funny?

They're the dry kind. Most of these designs are deadpan rather than loud — the joke lands on the second look rather than being shouted from across the street. If you want a shirt that makes one specific person laugh very hard rather than forty people smile politely, this is the right collection.

What size should I order?

Order your usual size for a normal fit, or size up once for the boxy, slightly oversized drape most people want from a graphic tee. Every listing shows the exact blank and full measurements, and there is a complete size guide.

Do funny graphic tees make good gifts?

They're one of the few gifts under $30 that read as thoughtful rather than last-minute, because a well-chosen joke proves you were paying attention. Pick the design that matches something specific about the person rather than the broadly funny one, and see the gift guide for shortlists. Every order ships free.

Graphic tees for women, without the script font

Somewhere along the line, women’s graphic tees got reduced to three jokes: wine, being tired, and being a mom who is tired because of wine. It’s a strangely small box for a category that used to be the most expressive thing in a wardrobe. Everything here is the opposite — designs with a point of view, drawn properly, printed on cotton worth keeping.

What makes these different

  • An actual aesthetic range. Pastel goth, pop-art cowgirl, disco-ball glam and retro smiley — not one tone repeated in four colourways.
  • Original illustration. Every graphic is drawn in-house. None of it is licensed, traced, or bought off a marketplace.
  • Soft, but not flimsy. Combed ring-spun and garment-dyed cotton that drapes properly instead of going sheer.
  • Funny without being cute. Delusional But Thriving and Hot Mess Express land because they’re self-aware rather than saccharine.

Who this is for

  • Anyone whose style is “mostly black with one strange thing”
  • Alt, goth and soft-grunge wardrobes that want colour without going twee
  • People buying for a sister, friend or partner with a very specific sense of humour
  • Y2K and cowgirl-aesthetic dressers looking for something not mass-produced
  • Women who want an oversized tee that reads intentional, not borrowed

How the unisex fit works on women

These are unisex cuts, which gives you two good options. Take your usual size for a relaxed, slightly oversized fit — the standard graphic-tee look, easy over leggings or tucked into denim. Or size down once for something closer to a women’s cut through the shoulder. The size guide publishes chest and body length for every blank so you can pick deliberately rather than hoping.

For outfit ideas, how to style weird graphic tees covers the contrast trick that stops a soft graphic reading twee, and the cowgirl aesthetic guide goes deeper on that particular look.

Shipping, quality & returns

  • Free shipping on every order, no minimum.
  • Made to order in the US — about 2–5 business days to print, 3–5 to arrive.
  • Soft-hand DTG printing with no stiff plastic panel across the front.
  • 14 days to make it right on anything wrong or damaged — returns.
  • Cold wash, inside out, hang dry. See the care guide.

Women's graphic tee FAQ

Are these women's cut or unisex?

Unisex cuts. Most women take their usual size for a relaxed, slightly oversized fit, or size down once for something closer through the shoulder. Every listing publishes chest and body-length measurements — see the size guide.

What are the softest shirts you make?

Bella + Canvas 3001 in Airlume combed cotton is smoothest, Gildan Softstyle is lightest, and Comfort Colors 1717 is garment-dyed so it starts heavy and softens with each wash. Each product page names its blank; full specs on the shirt guide.

Do you have plus sizes?

Most designs run to 3XL and several go to 4XL or 5XL depending on the blank. The available run is shown on each product page, and because the cuts are unisex the larger sizes are properly proportioned rather than scaled-up smalls.
